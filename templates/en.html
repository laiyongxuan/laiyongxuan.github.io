Data and intelligence for the real world

My research spans data management and artificial intelligence, with interests in AI agents, computer vision, intelligent transportation and edge computing. I connect algorithmic research with practical intelligent systems.

I am with the Department of Computer Science, School of Information, Renmin University of China, and the Fuzhou Research Institute, Renmin University of China. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Application Technology from Renmin University in 2009. I worked at the School of Informatics, Xiamen University from 2009 to September 2026, serving as a professor from 2019.
