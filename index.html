Data and intelligence for the real world

My research spans data management and artificial intelligence, with interests in AI agents, computer vision, intelligent transportation and edge computing. I connect algorithmic research with practical intelligent systems.

I am a Professor in the Department of Computer Science, School of Information, Renmin University of China, and am also affiliated with the Fuzhou Research Institute, Renmin University of China. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Application Technology from Renmin University in 2009. I worked at the School of Informatics, Xiamen University from 2009 to September 2026, serving as a professor from 2019.

Graduate study & research opportunities

Prospective graduate students interested in relevant programs at Renmin University of China are welcome to contact me. Undergraduate students are also welcome to discuss research interests and project ideas.

If you enjoy engineering, building systems and working on practical projects, I would be glad to hear from you. We seek research questions grounded in real needs, turn algorithms and models into useful, reliable systems, and evaluate and improve them in practice. Together, we can take research beyond publications, solve real problems and make a positive impact on the world.

Feel free to share an engineering project, an open-source contribution, a system prototype, or a practical problem you would like to solve with technology.

Please include your CV, academic background and project experience, together with a brief description of the research questions that interest you. Suggested subject: “Research / Graduate Inquiry + Name + University”.
